SECTION 1: TEST PLAN, OBJECTIVES & SCENARIOS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Core Objective:
  Benchmark interface intuitiveness, track interaction velocity, and uncover 
  navigation friction across all 6 core layout viewports of the RoamAI app.

* Success Benchmarks:
  - Minimum 90% Task Completion Rate (TCR) across multi-page workflows
  - System Usability Scale (SUS) score > 80 (Grade A)
  - Average Error Frequency < 1.5 mis-clicks per user session

* Testing Methodology:
  Remote, moderated testing utilizing live Figma cursor tracking and 
  "think-aloud" protocols over the 6 stitched interaction states.

* Test Scenarios Administered:
  - Scenario 1 (Onboarding & Configuration - Screens 1 & 2): 
    "Launch the app, select the 'Get Started' CTA, and input a luxury trip 
     destination to Paris, France for June 12-18, 2026."
  - Scenario 2 (Itinerary & Concierge Interaction - Screens 3 & 4): 
    "Review Day 1 schedule items (Eiffel Tower & Louvre), open the AI Chat 
     tab, and query the system for a photogenic cafe near the museum."
  - Scenario 3 (Saved Vault & Profile Assessment - Screens 5 & 6): 
    "Navigate to Saved Trips to review your item metrics, and open the profile 
     settings page to verify user account parameters."
